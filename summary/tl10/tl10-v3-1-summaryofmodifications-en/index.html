    Summary of Minor Correctios – TL10 version 3.1

    TL10 - In situ Formation of Exogenous Compounds version 3.1

    Summary of Modifications

    Following the withdrawal of the TL19 - Prednisone and Prednisolone, the comment in TL10 mentioning that the possible in situ formation of prednisone and prednisolone would be addressed in another Technical Letter has been removed from the Introduction section.

    The version 3.1 of TL10 becomes effective immediately.
