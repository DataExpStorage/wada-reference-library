    Summary of Modifications – ISL TD2027EPO

    Summary of Major Modifications

    This document summarizes the major modifications in the ISL TD2027EPO. In addition, a redline version against the TD2024EPO, which is the current version in force, is also presented for reference.

    Key updates and modifications include:

    1. Effective Date: The effective date is 01 January 2027.

    2 General Revisions:

    Reorganized and renumbered the document to consolidate common PAGE method specifications, analytical testing strategy, and evaluation/reporting provisions. Scientific references, figures, terminology and examples were also updated throughout.

    3 Introduction:

    Updated ERA terminology and definitions, including the use of dEPO for darbepoetin preparations and bEPO for endogenous EPO in blood, and revised examples for CERA, EPO-Fc, rEPO biosimilars and TGF-β signaling inhibitors.

    4 Analytical Testing for ERA(s):

    • Established a general requirement to follow ISL Article 5.3.3 for initial storage and Sample aliquoting of both "A" and "B" Samples, including the order of analysis when whole-blood testing and analysis of plasma fractions are both required.
    • Applied the recommendation to perform a requested ERA "B" Sample Confirmation Procedure within one (1) month of the "A" Sample AAF irrespective of Sample matrix.

    5 Pre-Analytical Procedure:

    Simplified the pre-analytical requirements by removing most matrix-specific centrifugation, storage and aliquoting instructions and relying on the ISL and, for DBS Samples, the ISL TD DBS, while retaining general measures intended to minimize ERA degradation.

    6 PAGE Analytical Methods' Specifications:

    • Specified that only 16-bit grayscale TIFF files may be used and that images in which the Sample of interest is overexposed shall not be used.
    • Made the use of 10% acrylamide (%T) gels mandatory for ERA separation by SAR-/SDS-PAGE.

    7 Analytical Testing Strategy:

    • Revised the PAGE testing strategy so that SAR- or SDS-PAGE shall be applied as the primary ITP for all ERAs. IEF-PAGE shall not be used as the primary ITP; it may be used as a complementary ITP for difficult-to-interpret CERA or EPO-Fc results, as an additional CP in specified circumstances, and for CERA/EPOFc as a CP where complementary ITP evidence allows definitive identification.
    • Strengthened "A" CP requirements for low-signal findings and Quality Controls; the Laboratory shall explore measures to improve an ERA signal that is too low for reliable identification; NQC sample(s) shall be p. 2 prepared in the Sample matrix with a clearly identifiable endogenous EPO signal; and PQC sample(s) shall be prepared in the Sample matrix with the ERA(s) under confirmation.
    • Revised "B" CP requirements by applying the "A" CP requirements subject to defined exceptions. If more than one PAGE CP was used for CERA or EPO-Fc in the "A" Sample, one conclusive PAGE CP is sufficient for the "B" Sample; where IEF-PAGE was added to SAR-/SDS-PAGE for an rEPO or dEPO "A" CP upon Expert request, only the applied SAR- or SDS-PAGE method is required for the "B" CP.
    • Removed the dedicated provisions for optional Test Sensitivity Controls (TSC), including the associated TSC-specific acceptance and repeat-analysis guidance.

    8 Evaluation and Interpretation of Results:

    • Expanded SAR-/SDS-PAGE interpretation guidance to address ERA- and biosimilar-dependent migration and band-width differences, mixed endogenous/rEPO bands and multiple ERA bands, supported by additional examples and figures.
    • Revised the IEF-PAGE rEPO identification criteria to apply when IEF-PAGE is used as a complementary CP and introduced a blood-specific densitometric criterion: the intensity of the two most intense bands in the basic area shall be at least twice that of the most intense band in the endogenous area.

    9 Reporting ERA Findings:

    • Established an explicit reporting pathway for Negative Findings that do not require second opinions, including negative ITP results and non-confirmed PAAFs. For non-confirmed PAAFs and findings with no endogenous or exogenous ERA band, the Laboratory shall recommend analysis of previously collected Samples or Target Testing and, where applicable, document the absence of ERA signals and possible degradation indicators in ADAMS.
    • Clarified that confirmed ERA findings require second opinions before reporting as an AAF or ATF, but that the Laboratory remains ultimately responsible for the reporting decision under its documented Management System procedures, including in the event of disagreement with the second opinion Experts.
    • Expanded rEPO reporting and follow-up provisions to interface with VAR-EPO investigations. An rEPO AAF may be withheld pending completion of a WADA-directed investigation, whereas an ATF may be reported before the VAR-EPO investigation is completed; follow-up actions include analysis of previously collected Samples and targeted blood/urine ERA and blood ABP testing.
    • Introduced requirements for Reporting of Tampering; where signs indicate that proteases were added to a Sample to induce ERA degradation and mask ERA Use, the Laboratory shall report an AAF for Tampering and comment in ADAMS on the possible impact on ERA stability.

    10 Annex A - Examples of PAGE Analysis of Different ERA Biosimilars:

    Added a dedicated annex with SAR-PAGE and IEF-PAGE examples for different dEPO, rEPO and EPO-Fc preparations/biosimilars to support interpretation of migration patterns and band variability.

    11 Annex B - ERA Second Opinion Procedure:

    • Expanded the documentation to be uploaded through the WADA-assigned access-restricted data management system, including specified Sample details, raw TIFF images, processed GASepo data and the Annex C summary template, with email confirmation to WADA that the necessary documents have been uploaded.

    p. 3

    • Revised second-opinion timelines and responsibilities; Experts should confirm acceptance/rejection within two (2) business days; additional analyses/information may be requested within three (3) business days; absent such a request, opinions are due within five (5) business days; Laboratories have seven (7) business days for requested analyses or three (3) business days for information-only requests; and final opinions are due within five (5) business days of complete documentation.
    • Established a consultation mechanism for exceptional or rare ERA migration profiles. The consultation involves three EPO Working Group Experts, normally occurs within five (5) business days of the request, and requires a unanimous final opinion; absent unanimity, the finding should be reported as an ATF.
    • Clarified that determination of whether an Athlete carries the EPO c.577del variant is outside of the second opinion providers' evaluation and shall be concluded by the Laboratory performing the relevant procedure. Where a rEPO second opinion remains contingent on VAR-EPO status, the Expert opinion shall include an appropriate disclaimer.

    12 Annex C - Template for Second Opinion Requests on ERA Analysis:

    Expanded and restructured the second-opinion request template, to be completed in English, with detailed ITP and CP fields for Sample metadata, raw image/exposure data, Sample volume, immunopurification, internal standards, electrophoretic method, QC details and lane numbers, blotting/detection reagents and additional comments.

    13 Annex D - EPO c.577del Variant:

    • Expanded the analytical characterization of VAR-EPO and revised the rEPO testing strategy and Table D1 for blood and urine, including interpretation of heterozygous double-band and homozygous higher-molecular-weight single-band patterns.
    • Added specific "B" CP rules; once the absence of VAR-EPO has been established in the "A" Sample, VAR-EPO testing does not need to be repeated during the "B" CP; if "A" Sample VAR-EPO analysis was subcontracted, the rEPO "B" CP shall remain with the Laboratory that performed the rEPO "A" CP unless otherwise approved by WADA.
    • Established an optional Reverse-Normal Immunopurification (RNI) confirmation pathway. A finding is reported as a Negative Finding if the RNI CP confirms VAR-EPO; if the CP confirms rEPO in the absence of VAR-EPO, the finding proceeds to the Second Opinion Procedure. RNI may be subcontracted, in consultation with the TA, to a Laboratory with the method in its ISO/IEC 17025 Scope of Accreditation, with costs borne by the TA.
    • Established a revised decision and investigation pathway following a regular SAR-/SDS-PAGE rEPO CP based on known or unknown VAR-EPO carrier status. Where status remains unresolved, the revised process prioritizes DNA/RNI analysis in available previously collected blood Samples, and otherwise provides for an ATF pending follow-up blood and urine ERA testing; the follow-up collection timeframe is shortened to approximately two (2) weeks.
    • Expanded DNA testing requirements for identification of VAR-EPO carriers.

    14 Annex E:

    Added a flow-chart diagram as general guidance for investigations into possible VAR-EPO expression.
