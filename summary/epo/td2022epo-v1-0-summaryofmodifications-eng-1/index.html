    Summary of Modifications – TD2022EPO

    TD2022EPO version 1.0

    Summary of Major Modifications

    The Technical Document on Harmonization of Analysis and Reporting of Erythropoietin (EPO) and other Erythropoietin-Receptor Agonists (ERAs) by Polyacrylamide Gel Electrophoretic (PAGE) Analytical Methods, TD2022EPO version 1.0, has been modified to include a new Annex B, which describes the existence of a minor EPO gene variant that is present in a minor percentage (< 1%) of individuals with East Asian ancestry and provides further guidance for the management and reporting of recombinant EPO (recEPO) findings.

    The TD2022EPO version 1.0 replaces the former TD2021EPO version 2.0 and becomes effective on 1 January 2022.
