    Summary of Modifications – ISL TD2027GH

    Summary of Major Modifications

    This document summarizes the major modifications in the ISL TD2027GH. In addition, a redline version against the TD2021GH, which is the current version in force, is also presented for reference.

    Key updates and modifications include:

    1. Effective Date: The effective date is 01 January 2027.

    2 Principle of the Test Method:

    Transitioned the hGH Isoforms Differential Immunoassays from the coated tube (CT) platform to a microtiter plate (MTP) format, including a reduction in serum Aliquot volume from 50 µL/tube to 25 µL/well and use of an appropriate MTP luminometer. The capture and detection antibodies, reagents and detection system remain essentially unchanged.

    3 Pre-Analytical Procedure:

    • Revised and consolidated the validation acceptance criteria in new Table 1. The LOQ acceptance criterion for Rec1, Pit1, Rec2 and Pit2 is ≤ 0.100 ng/mL (previously ≤ 0.050 ng/mL), and the within-Laboratory Repeatability (sᵣ) and Intermediate Precision (sw) criteria changed from ≤ 15% and ≤ 20% to sᵣ < 15% and sw < 20%, respectively. The applicable minimum requirements for Quantitative Procedures in ISL TD VAL are expressly incorporated.
    • Added verification, when needed, of the suitability of using the same calibration curve for different plates from the same kit lot analyzed on the same day.
    • Removed the former comment that Laboratory LOQs established from Test Method performance criteria should not be lower than the corresponding LOQs established by the kit manufacturer.

    4 Assay Pre-Analytical Procedure:

    Revised the whole-blood receipt check to reference the International Standard for Testing (IST) and to require the Laboratory to check that Samples have been kept refrigerated and have not been frozen following collection and during transportation to the Laboratory.

    5 Analytical Testing Strategy:

    • Added ITP Quality Control provisions; each ITP analysis should include the kits' positive and negative QC samples or, alternatively, Laboratory iQC samples prepared at an approximate Rec/Pit ratio of 1.50-2.50. When iQCs are used, their performance shall be followed using QC charts.
    • Revised CP repeat criteria by requiring the Laboratory to define in its SOP the circumstances in which the CP shall be repeated.
    • Made use of well-characterized and stable iQC samples mandatory for verification of CP performance and specified that the iQC sample(s) should be selected based on the ITP result, such as the iQC with the closest Rec concentration to that measured for the Sample in the ITP.
    • Established verification requirements for new kit lots using the Laboratory's iQCs and QC charts. Deviations of more than 20% between lots in measured Rec and Pit concentrations trigger an internal p. 2 investigation; if impaired performance of the new kit lot is confirmed, the Laboratory shall inform WADA immediately.

    6 Interpretation of Test Results:

    • Revised the calculation sequence for Rec/Pit ratios; calculate the mean Rec and Pit concentrations from replicate Sample Aliquots, truncate the mean concentrations to three decimal places, calculate the Rec/Pit ratio using those truncated values, and compare the ratio truncated to two decimal places with the applicable sex- and kit-specific Decision Limit (DL).
    • Specified that when the mean Pit concentration is below the Laboratory-validated LOQ for Pit, the corresponding LOQ_Pit value, expressed to three decimal places, is used to calculate the Rec/Pit ratio.
    • Consolidated and revised the decision rules in new Table 3, explicitly incorporating the 0.150 ng/mL Rec concentration criterion into the ITP and CP classifications for Negative Findings, PAAFs, ATFs and AAFs, as applicable to each kit.

    7 Reporting of Test Results:

    • Expanded AAF/ATF Test Report requirements to include the mean Rec and Pit concentrations for each kit calculated from the triplicate determinations, in addition to the Rec/Pit ratios, uc values and applicable DLs. The Laboratory Documentation Package shall include the Rec and Pit concentrations for each of the three Sample Aliquots analyzed using Kit 1 and Kit 2.
    • Clarified that when the mean Pit concentration is below LOQ_Pit and the Laboratory-validated LOQ_Pit is used to calculate the Rec/Pit ratio, the ratio is reported as a "greater than" (>) value.

    8 Relative Combined Standard Uncertainty:

    • Revised the iQC-based approach for estimating measurement uncertainty. iQCs shall be prepared at different Rec and Pit concentration levels using at least three dilutions to yield an approximate Rec/Pit ratio of 1.50-2.50 and should be measured at least in triplicate over six days under reproducibility conditions, covering the working range of the assays and levels close to the DLs.
    • Simplified the former detailed measurement-uncertainty calculation framework by removing the fixed four-concentration design and the alternative long-term QC1/QC2 calculation approach, while directing the Laboratory generally to ISL TD DL for estimation of assay measurement uncertainty.

    9 Detection of Synthetic Long-acting GH Analogues:

    • Added a new section addressing synthetic Long-Acting GH Analogues (LAGH), including eftansomatropin-α, redalsomatropin-α, somapacitan and somatrogon, and recommending specific LC-MS Test Methods because of the structural differences of these preparations.
    • Specified that Kit 2 may also serve as an ITP for some LAGH preparations. When a PAAF obtained with Kit 2 is not confirmed with Kit 1 during the CP, the Laboratory shall consider LC-MS analysis as a CP for LAGH detection, either internally or by subcontracting to another Laboratory in consultation with and after approval by the Testing Authority.
