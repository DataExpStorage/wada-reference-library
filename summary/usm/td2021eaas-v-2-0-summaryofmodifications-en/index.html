    Summary of Modifications – TD2021EAAS version 2.0

    TD2021EAAS version 2.0

    Summary of Major Modifications

    The Technical Document on Measuring and Reporting of Endogenous Anabolic Androgenic Steroid (EAAS) Markers of the Urinary Steroid Profile, TD2021EAAS version 2.0, has been corrected to remove the misquotation of specific TD2021IRMS articles under Articles 3.3.1 and 3.3.2. Instead, a general reference to the effective TD IRMS is provided.

    In addition, it has been clarified under Article 3.3.2, that the Laboratory shall also report the measured specific gravity of the "B" Sample.
