    Summary of Modifications – ISL TD2027CG/LH

    Summary of Major Modifications

    This document summarizes the major modifications in the ISL TD2027CG/LH. In addition, a redline version against the TD2021CG/LH, which is the current version in force, is also presented for reference.

    Key updates and modifications include:

    1. Effective Date: The effective date is 01 January 2027.

    2. General Revisions: The Technical Document was reorganized, including the following sections: Introduction, Analytical Testing, Interpretation/Reporting, and Results Management, Annex 1 (Medical Evaluation of a Case with Confirmed Positive hCG Test; standardized terminology and cross-references; and clarified that the TD constitutes an integral part of the ISL.

    3 Pre-Analytical Procedure:

    • Changed the requirement for refrigerated Aliquots to reach room temperature before instrument loading from "should" to "shall".
    • Specified that, when the "A" Sample Confirmation Procedure cannot be performed within seven (7) days, the "A" Sample should be transferred to deep-freezing storage (-70 °C or less) until the Confirmation Procedure is conducted.

    4 Analytical Testing for hCG:

    • Made the Initial Testing Procedure Quality Control requirement more specific by requiring a urine QC sample containing α/β-heterodimeric hCG; added that the 6th International Standard for Intact hCG (NIBSC 18/244) may be used for preparation of hCG QCs.
    • Expanded validation requirements by specifying that the minimum validation requirements for Quantitative Procedures in ISL TD VAL apply to the hCG Analytical Testing Procedure and that the Table 1 acceptance criteria also apply to Initial Testing Procedures targeting total or α/β-heterodimeric hCG.
    • Revised the Confirmation Procedure QC requirements: the positive urine QC sample is now defined as having a concentration greater than the corresponding Decision Limit, instead of 6-15 IU/L, and the QC used for the Initial Testing Procedure may also be used as the positive QC.
    • Specified that, when a Laboratory lacks the analytical capacity to perform the hCG Confirmation Procedure, shipment and analysis by another Laboratory requires consultation with and approval by the responsible Testing Authority.

    5 Analytical Testing for LH:

    • Revised the total LH QC concentration range from 5-50 IU/L to 5-60 IU/L and made preparation of the QC sample in urine a mandatory requirement.

    6 hCG Results:

    • Replaced the detailed Technical Document provisions on Therapeutic Use Exemption enquiries following an hCG PAAF with a reference to ISL Article 5.3.4.1.3 c, including removal of the TD-specific requirements concerning written Testing Authority/Results Management Authority instructions and provision of TUE information to WADA.

    p. 2

    7 LH Results:

    • Revised ADAMS reporting instructions for LH: removed the TD-specific instruction to report the measured total LH concentration at the PAAF stage; specified that the measured concentration is not reported for Negative Findings; and specified that ATFs are reported with the measured total LH concentration after specific-gravity adjustment. AAF reporting now expressly refers to the estimated concentration of total LH and retains reporting to three significant figures.

    8 Results Management:

    • Clarified the timing reference for recommended follow-up no-notice Testing as following the Laboratory's reporting of the hCG AAF/Negative Finding or LH ATF, rather than following the initial finding.
    • For hCG follow-up Testing, added that follow-up Samples may include urine and serum Samples collected during the same Sample Collection Session.
