    Summary of Modifications – ISL TD2027HEM

    Summary of Major Modifications

    This document summarizes the major modifications in the ISL TD2027HEM version 1.0. In addition, a redline version against the TD2021BAR v2.0, which is the current version in force, is also presented for reference.

    Key updates and modifications include:

    • Effective Date: 01 January 2027.
    • General Revisions: Reorganized the Technical Document to separate requirements for whole blood Sample reception, instrument performance and Quality Control, EQAS, whole blood Sample analysis, and reporting. Terminology was also aligned throughout with the Hematological Module of the ABP and the current ISL framework.

    1.0 Introduction: Expanded the stated purpose to cover both the analysis and reporting of whole blood Samples for the Hematological Module of the ABP and aligned the scope terminology for Laboratories and applicable accreditation requirements.

    2.0 Whole Blood Sample Reception and Timing of Analysis:

    • Reference to the analysis of Samples collected as per the International Standard for Testing (IST).
    • Clarification that the Samples are to be maintained at a cool temperature until the start of Sample homogenization.
    • Added that Laboratories may receive whole blood Samples as an "A" Sample only or as "A" and "B" Samples, provided they are collected in approved tubes, sealed, and transported in compliance with the International Standard for Testing (IST).
    • Revised the timing requirement to expressly permit analysis beyond twelve (12) hours if the Laboratory receives information from the Sample Collection Authority regarding collection and transportation conditions which projects an acceptable Blood Stability Score (BSS).
    • Clarified Sample handling before analysis, including maintaining whole blood Samples at approximately 4°C when immediate analysis is not possible and keeping the temperature data logger with the Sample(s) until the start of homogenization.
    • Clarified that if there are irregularities observed with a Sample, the Laboratory shall proceed with the analysis, as per the ISL, to ensure it is conducted with an acceptable Blood Stability Score (BSS).

    · 3.0 Instrument Performance and Quality Control

    • Described the current manufacturer's Quality Control (QC) samples and QC reporting platform, and instructions on the Laboratory's utilization, review and retention of the manufacturer's QC data and reports.
    • Added a requirement to analyze the QC samples at least once a week during routine operational periods when Sample analyses have not been conducted.
    • Revised the fresh whole blood sample analysis requirements, including changing the frequency from at least monthly to at least quarterly (four times per year), prohibiting the use of Athlete Samples, and specifying requirements for QC verification, sample homogenization and repeatability assessment.

    p. 2

    · 4.0 External Quality Assessment Scheme

    • Expanded External Quality Assessment Scheme (EQAS) requirements by specifying that each instrument used for whole blood Sample analysis in support of the Hematological Module of the ABP shall be included in the EQAS.
    • Clarified QC requirements prior to EQAS sample analysis and that EQAS sample handling and reporting shall be conducted as instructed by the EQAS provider.
    • Added a requirement to download and store the raw temperature profile report when a temperature data logger is included with an EQAS sample shipment.

    · 5.0 Analysis of Athlete Biological Passport Blood Samples

    • Specified that the valid lot of QC samples (levels 1, 2 and 3) shall be analyzed in duplicate before whole blood Sample analysis and an out-of-range QC level shall be reanalyzed in duplicate.
    • Included clearer Sample analysis requirements for the manufacturer's QC samples, including mid-session QC requirements, QC requirements for larger batches, and end-of-session QC verification; including evaluation via QC-charts.
    • Added that, if Sample volume is exhausted before the duplicate-analysis acceptance criteria can be met, the Sample shall be reported as "Not Analyzed" and the issue described in ADAMS.

    · 6.0 Reporting

    • Included a requirement to retain the data logger temperature reports for 10 years.
    • Clarification that data logger temperature reports shall be uploaded into ADAMS as soon as possible, and a reporting comment when upload is not possible.
    • Clarified that whole blood Sample analysis results shall be reported in ADAMS as soon as possible after analysis and should be reported within three (3) days after Sample reception.
    • Simplified the reporting requirements by referencing the reporting requirements of the ISL, TDs, and ADAMS.
