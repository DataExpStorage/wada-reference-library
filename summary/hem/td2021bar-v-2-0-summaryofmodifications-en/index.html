    Summary of Modifications – TD2021BAR version 2.0

    TD2021BAR version 2.0

    Summary of Major Modifications

    The Technical Document on Analytical Requirements for the Hematological Module of the Athlete Biological Passport, TD2021BAR version 2.0, has been corrected as follows:

    • The reference in the comment of section 6.1 of the TD2021BAR version 2.0 has been updated from ISTI 2020 Annex K, Article K.2.4 to ISTI Annex I, Article I.2.7.
