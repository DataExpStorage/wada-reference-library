    Summary of Changes to the International Standard for the Protection of Privacy and Personal Information (ISPPPI)

    a. Annex A, Module 3 (start point for retention of TUE certificates)

    Stakeholders brought to WADA's attention that the starting point for the retention period for TUE certificates and TUE rejection decision forms under Annex A was not clear and could be wrongly interpreted as beginning on the 'approval date' of the certificate/decision. 'Certificate date' was intended to refer to the date of TUE expiry, not approval. This was an intentional change from the 2018 version of the ISPPPI to ensure evidence of the TUE is retained for as long as an athlete sample, collected during the period of validity of the TUE, can be re-analysed. Otherwise, an athlete could receive an ADRV for a substance for which they had received a valid exemption at the time the sample was collected.     b. Annex A, Module 7 (start point for retention of ABP-related documentation

    Similarly, Athlete Passport Management Units (APMUs) advised WADA that the current retention period start date for ABP-related documentation could be interpreted as requiring documentation packages to be redacted to remove ABP results that are more than 10 years old. This interpretation would be operationally impractical and would undermine the integrity of the APMU analysis produced at the time the ABP documentation package was created. It would also not be aligned with the retention period for laboratory reports provided for in Module 5 of Annex A to the ISPPPI.
