    Summary of Modifications – ISL TD2027LCOC

    Summary of Major Modifications

    This document summarizes the major modifications in the ISL TD2027LCOC. In addition, a redline version against the TD2021LCOC, which is the current version in force, is also presented for reference.

    Key updates and modifications include:

    • Effective Date: The effective date will be 01 January 2027.
    • General Revisions: to enhance clarity and readability and update formatting.

    1.0 Introduction: Clarifies that the Laboratory Chain of Custody (LCOC) starts at Sample reception up to, and including, disposal or secondary use.

    2.0 Laboratory Chain of Custody:

    • Further clarification that the LCOC includes authorized Persons and controlled and defined storage areas.
    • Definition of "forensic corrections" describing how handwritten corrections shall be recorded in Laboratory documentation.

    · 2.1 Maintaining Laboratory Chain of Custody

    • Removed the "line of sight" requirement when the Samples are located in a secure, restricted, and defined control zone of the Laboratory during Sample handling.
    • Expanded the maintenance of chain of custody to include resulting extracts from Aliquots.
    • Chain of custody is maintained on analytical instrumentation within the control zone of a laboratory.
    • Chain of custody is maintained in external Sample storage facility under the responsibility of the Laboratory.

    · 2.2 Recording Laboratory Chain of Custody during Analytical Testing

    • Sample LCOC is recorded until disposal or secondary purposes as per the ISL.
    • The recording of the courier tracking number (if provided) has been removed.
    • Aliquots LCOC is recorded from Aliquot preparation to completion of analysis/disposal.
    • Aliquots LCOC to include any unused portion remaining in the Laboratory.
    • Electronic LCOC needs functionality to record unique identifiers of Persons/Locations with custody and record LCOC at the moment a change in custody occurs.
    • Electronic LCOC shall have Audit Trail and data integrity capabilities as defined in this Article.

    · 2.3 Recording Chain of Custody for Sample Transfers

    • Transfers to other Laboratories may include Samples and/or Aliquots.
