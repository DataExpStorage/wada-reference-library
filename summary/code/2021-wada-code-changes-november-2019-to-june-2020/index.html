    Editions: LIST OF CHANGES TO THE WORLD ANTI-DOPING CODE SINCE THE WORLD CONFERENCE IN BUSAN · 2027 Code & IS Update Process: Summary of Major Changes – World Anti-Doping Code · 2021 World Anti-Doping Code – Changes from November 2019 to June 2020

    p. 1

    2021 World Anti-Doping Code – Changes from November 2019 to June 2020

    1. Any references to numbers (for example, references to days or years) has been put in the following format: written number (#). Example: ten (10) days; or, six (6) years.

    2 Page 2:

    A change was made to show that the World Anti-Doping Code has been amended four times.

    3 Introduction:

    In paragraph three, the following has been removed to be consistent with the requirements in Article 20 as approved in Katowice: "…and volunteers of Signatories…."

    p. 2

    In the title, removed "..or Hearing Process" because Results Management encompasses hearings.

    Throughout the entire paragraph, added "or data" after Sample to be consistent with rest of the Article.

    p. 3

    7. Comment to Article 7 - Footnote 45:

    Reference to Article 14.2.2. and 14.3.2 have been changed to Article 14 and Article 14.3.

    Removed "…and the conduct of hearings" and replaced it with "purposes" because Results Management encompasses hearings.

    Reference to Article 14.2 have been changed to Article 14.

    p. 4

    Reference to Article 14.2.1 have been changed to Article 14.

    Reference to Article 14.5.3 have been changed to Article 14.3.

    11. Comment to Article 8.5 - Footnote 54:

    The text of Article 8.5 was changed before Katowice but the corresponding Comment was inadvertently left unchanged. The Comment has been modified to be consistent with the text.

    • "..that wants to" was replaced with "may".
    • "a party or as…. may condition its approval of a single hearing on being granted that right." Was removed.

    The word "elimination" was added to be consistent with the referenced Articles.

    p. 5

    "..or a Specified Method" was added after Specified Substance to be consistent with the addition of Specified Method in Article 4.2.

    "Complicity or Attempted" was added to include the full name of the referenced Article consistent with the other referenced Articles.

    p. 6

    The International Standard referenced in this article was changed from the ISTI to the ISL (International Standard for Laboratories).

    Reference to Article 14.2 was changed to Article 14.

    p. 7

    Added "...an Athlete or other…" to be consistent with language used in rest of Article

    1. Article 10.9.3.4: 10.9.3.

    p. 8

    19. Comment to Article 13 - Footnote 82:

    Replaced "federations" by "National Federations".

    Replaced "national-level appeal" with "appellate" to be consistent with terminology in Article 15.1.

    Reference to Article 14.2 was replaced with Article 14.

    p. 9

    Added "… or other Person's" to be consistent with language used in rest of Article 14. Replaced the reference of ISTI to ISRM (International Standard for Results Management).

    23. Comment to Article 14.6 - Footnote 93:

    Changed "will" to "should" to be consistent with principle and language in Article 22 that the Signatories have certain expectations for actions by governments but governments are bound only by the relevant international intergovernmental treaties.

    Removed "..by WADA".

    p. 10

    p. 11

    Added WADA "-accredited or.."

    Added WADA-accredited "or approved"

    p. 12

    Article 24.1.12.1 (e) has been removed from 24.1.12.1 and is now a stand-alone provision under 24.1.12 (i.e. Article 24.1.12.2). Therefore, the provisions after 24.1.12.2 have been re-numbered.

    "or methods" has been added after "substances".

    • "or Prohibited Method" has been added after "Prohibited Substance" to be consistent with the addition of Specified Method in Article 4.2.

    p. 13

    Definitions

    Aggravating Circumstances:

    "or the hearing process" was removed because hearings are encompassed in Results Management.

    Anti-Doping Activities:

    "hearings" was removed because hearings are encompassed in Results Management.

    Athlete Support Personnel:

    p. 14

    Doping Control:

    "hearings and appeals" was removed because hearings and appeals are encompassed in Results Management.

    National Anti-Doping Organization:

    "of hearings" was removed because hearings are encompassed in Results Management.

    No Significant Fault or Negligence:

    Comment to Tampering:

    "and hearing" was removed because hearings are encompassed in Results Management.
