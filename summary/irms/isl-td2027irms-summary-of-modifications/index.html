    Summary of Modifications – ISL TD2027IRMS

    Summary of Major Modifications

    This document summarizes the major modifications in the ISL TD2027IRMS. In addition, a redline version against the TD2022IRMS, which is the current version in force, is also presented for reference.

    Key updates and modifications include:

    1. Effective Date: The effective date is 01 January 2027.

    2 General Revisions:

    The document has been reorganized into distinct articles for 1) Scope, 2) Introduction, 3) Application of Gas Chromatography / Combustion / Isotope Ratio Mass Spectrometry (GC/C/IRMS), 4) Validation Requirements, 5) Analysis Requirements, 6) Results Interpretation, 7) "B" Sample Confirmation Procedure, and 8) Reporting of Results. Terminology, references, equations, tables, and the Endogenous Reference Compound (ERC) selection - results interpretation diagram were updated throughout.

    3 Introduction:

    • Expanded the GC/C/IRMS framework to include the comparison of carbon isotope ratios between ERCs when assessing whether an ERC may have been affected by the administration of a metabolic precursor.
    • Included the mandatory validation of a third ERC and specified that metabolically related ERCs shall not be combined where two (2) ERCs are required for a conclusive interpretation of results.

    4 Application of GC/C/IRMS:

    • Specified Target Compounds (TC) selection priorities for the urinary steroid Markers. Testosterone (T), 5α-androstane-3α,17β-diol (5αAdiol) and 5β-androstane-3α,17β-diol (5βAdiol) are the most responsive TCs for detecting T administration; androsterone (A), and etiocholanolone (Etio) shall be analyzed when those primary criteria cannot be applied or produce an Atypical Finding (ATF).
    • GC/C/IRMS analysis of epitestosterone (E) is required after negative or inconclusive results for T and its Metabolites when E exceeds 100 ng/mL in female Samples or 200 ng/mL in male Samples (SG-adjusted where necessary), increasing the female trigger cut-off from 50 ng/mL to 100 ng/mL.
    • Established that GC/C/IRMS for other Prohibited Substances is a non-mandatory Analytical Testing Procedure (ATP) and expanded the scope to include 1-en-steroids, 7β-hydroxydehydroepiandrosterone (7β-OH-DHEA), and dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA). Laboratories without inhouse capacity shall, where GC/C/IRMS is required, confirm the finding before subcontracting the analysis in consultation with the Testing Authority (TA) .
    • Aligned concentration-based decisions for 19-Norandrosterone (19-NA), 19-noretiocholanolone (19-NE), boldenone (B), 17β-hydroxy-5β-androst-1-en-3-one (BM1) and formestane (F) with the ISL TD2027MRL.
    • Introduced substance-specific testing and reporting instructions for 1-en-steroids and 7β-OH-DHEA; referred 19-NA/19-NE decisions to the applicable ISL TD NA; and added DHEA as an additional TC to assist Results Management in distinguishing synthetic T Use from administration of its precursors.
    • Revised the B/BM1 and F decision pathways:

    p. 2

    • ꟷ B/BM1 at concentrations below (<) 2.5 ng/mL are now reported as a Negative Finding unless GC/C/IRMS is performed (rather than as an ATF).
    • ꟷ For B/BM1 concentrations above (>) 30 ng/mL and extensive Sample degradation, GC/C/IRMS is now required; the finding shall be reported as an ATF if the GC/C/IRMS analysis is not possible.
    • ꟷ For F at a concentration above (>) 150 ng/mL, extensive Sample degradation now results in an ATF.

    5 GC/C/IRMS Validation Requirements:

    • Increased the minimum validated ERCs from two (2) to three (3): PD as ERC₁ and at least two (2) additional ERCs.
    • Cholesterol (CHOL) was added to the selectable ERCs, and the document requires the same ERCs to be used for Samples and QC samples as well as during the "A" and "B" Confirmation Procedures.
    • Revised the Limit of Quantification (LOQ) and uc_Max requirements in Table 1 to cover the expanded TC scope. New entries include 1-en-steroids, 19-NA, 19-NE, DHEA, 7β-OH-DHEA and 7β-OH-EpiA; the E LOQ was increased from 50 ng/mL to 100 ng/mL
    • Expanded the reference-population requirements in Table 2 to the additional TCs. The criteria now include 7β-OH-DHEA and DHEA.

    6 GC/C/IRMS Analysis Requirements:

    • Broadened the derivatization provisions beyond acetylation while retaining the requirement to report values equivalent to underivatized compounds. The recommended δ¹³C coverage for the calibration steroid mixture has been revised.
    • An exception has been established from the use of a Negative Quality Control (NQC) sample containing some of the TCs (1-en-steroids, 6α-OH-AD, 19-NA, 19-NE, B, BM1 and F), with further clarifications of the reasons.

    7 Interpretation of GC/C/IRMS Results:

    • Established detailed negative-result logic for ERCs potentially affected by synthetic precursors. When δ¹³C(ERC₁) is below -25.0 ‰, the Laboratory shall assess another ERC; compatible ERCs are defined by ∆δ¹³C(ERC₁-ERC2) ≤ 2.0 ‰, while an ERC₁ result shall be rejected when the ERC δ¹³ values differ by more than (>) 2.0 ‰.
    • Specified that a positive intermediate result (i.e., obtained with one (1) ERC) is acceptable only when the ERC used has not been affected by a precursor, and that an Adverse Analytical Finding (AAF) requires positive results with two (2) compatible ERCs not affected by synthetic precursors.
    • Expanded the positive decision rules by establishing a greater-than (>) 3.0 ‰ criterion for ERC-TC pairs with 7β-OH-DHEA, DHEA, 19-NA or 19-NE, and a greater-than (>) 4.0 ‰ criterion for ERC-TC pairs with 1-en-steroids and 7β-OH-EpiA.
    • Clarified intermediate inconclusive and ATF outcomes, including cases where only one ERC-TC pair in a combined positivity criterion is met, technical limitations preventing reliable measurements, a valid substitute ERC producing an inconclusive result, compatible ERCs producing inconsistent results, or insufficient Sample volume preventing the confirmation with a second ERC.

    p. 3

    8 "B" Sample Confirmation Procedure:

    Consolidated the "B" Sample confirmation requirements and specified that, for a GC/C/IRMS-based AAF, at least the same TCs and ERCs that led to the "A" Sample AAF shall be measured during the "B" Confirmation Procedure. Added that if the "A" Sample AAF is based solely on the estimated TC concentration without GC/C/IRMS analysis, the "B" Sample needs only to confirm the presence and identity of the TC, at any concentration, for the AAF to remain valid.

    9 Reporting GC/C/IRMS Results:

    • Revised ADAMS reporting requirements to include the δ¹³C values of all TCs and ERCs needed to draw the conclusion, truncated to one decimal place, with each associated uc reported to two (2) significant figures rather than one decimal place. This replaces the former direction to report only ERC₁ for certain Negative Findings or ATFs.
    • Added recommending Results Management follow-up for GC/C/IRMS ATFs, including the review of the Athlete's blood and urinary ABP steroid profiles, Target Testing for GC/C/IRMS and the retrospective analysis of Samples kept in long-term storage.

    10 Annex A - Selection and Use of Endogenous Reference Compounds:

    Revised the decision diagram to reflect the ERC hierarchy, verification of ERC compatibility, and the resulting conclusion of results as Negative Finding*, AAF*, or ATF.
