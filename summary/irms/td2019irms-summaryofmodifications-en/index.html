    Summary of Modifications – TD2019IRMS

    TD2019IRMS:Summary of Major Modifications

    The Technical Document on Detection of Synthetic Forms of Endogenous Anabolic Androgenic Steroids by GC-C-IRMS, TD2019IRMS, has been modified to update the formula for adjustment of concentrations in Samples with high (> 1.018) urinary specific gravity (SG).

    The formula in footnote 3 has been updated to reflect the changes made in the new TD2019DL.

    In addition, the requirement to include the confirmed concentrations of the Markers of the steroid profile in the Test Report for IRMS has been removed, since the reporting of such values is mandatory within the steroid profile module in ADAMS.

    The TD2019IRMS replaces the former TD2016IRMS.
