About this library

The texts were converted from the PDFs published by WADA and proofread against them. Each page states when and from which address on wada-ama.org its PDF was retrieved; for the current documents the PDF used was verified to be byte-identical to WADA's copy on that date. In case of doubt, consult the original PDF.

Editions

All editions are kept. The current edition is shown by default; the edition menu and the "Editions" line switch to earlier ones (the article you are reading is kept where it exists).

Cross-references

References to articles, annexes and other documents are links. A reference to another document points to the edition that was in force when the citing edition took effect, so earlier editions link to the texts valid at their time. Dotted links are references to the Code inferred from an article number alone. Defined terms link to their definition; "Cited by" lists the passages in other documents and editions that refer to an article.

Conventions

  • "p. N" marks where a page of the PDF begins.
  • Tables are shown as tables and can be downloaded as CSV; figures and formulas are reproduced as images or as typeset formulas.
  • Obvious errors in the PDFs are kept as printed.
