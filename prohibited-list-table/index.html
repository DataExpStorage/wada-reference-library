Prohibited List 2027 – substances, testing and limits

Every substance and method named in the 2027 Prohibited List, one row per matrix, with the analytical testing category from TD2027ATP, the MRPL, MRL and Decision Limit from the Technical Documents, and further requirements from the Technical Documents and Letters. Explicit = the TD names the substance; implicit = inherited from the substance's class or group. Unofficial reference table – the authoritative texts are WADA's PDFs; every value links to its source.

How to read the table
  • ATP category (TD2027ATP): Routine OOC and INC menu – mandatory for all Samples (Table 1A); Routine INC menu – mandatory for all In-Competition Samples (1B); Routine menu – specific sports (1C); Special analysis (all laboratories) – additional mandatory ATP, applied on request or as a mandatory confirmation (Table 2); Special analysis (some laboratories) – non-mandatory ATP (Table 3) or an exception listed as not mandatory in Tables 1/2; Not listed in TD ATP for this matrix – no ATP for the matrix (for DBS also: Table 3 footnote 9, DBS only for Non-Threshold Substances without MRL).
  • MRPL from TD MRPL (urine, plasma/serum) and TD DBS (DBS); for S6–S9 the MRPL equals the MRL. MRL from TD MRL; Threshold/DL from TD DL, TD CG/LH and TD GH.
  • TD/TL notes: food or meat contamination, permitted medications, in-situ formation, interferences, GC/C/IRMS, second opinions, sex-specific rules, ATF investigations – open a row for the details and links.
  • Rows marked TD ATP group (e.g. steroid esters, ITPP, cDNA transfer, siRNA, homologous blood transfusion, Sample substitution/adulteration) and TD ATP marker (ABP Steroidal, Endocrine and Hematological modules, DEHP phthalates) are named in TD2027ATP but not in the List; they are shown for clarity.
  • The List is not exhaustive ("including, but not limited to"): a substance missing here can still be prohibited.
